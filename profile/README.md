# LLM Policy

I don't make any guarantee necessarily that agents haven't touched something, but I keep things in two broad piles;
stuff I care about and stuff I want. By cases:

The stuff I want but don't care about I vibecode.
The stuff I care about but don't want (to do) I script, with varying degrees of agent assistance or involvement.
The stuff I care about and I want I do myself.

To me, that means proving the theorems, doing exercises, and learning math; as well as thinking about how all that math
works together.

It means _not_ learning just enough (to later forget) of lua to fix some tangential editor issue. It means not doing six
hours of typing to refactor every theorem every time I want to tweak something on atlas. It means being able to not do
the stuff that I simply do not care to do. That is to stay, stuff I do not care about and do not want (to do), I don't
do, and the places you will see LLMs used most are those places.
